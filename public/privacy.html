Privacy

Hydrasynth Editor · last updated 11 October 2026

In short: the editor runs entirely in your browser. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics or tracking, and it loads nothing from other sites. Your patches, backups and recordings never leave your computer.

The only personal data involved is your IP address, which GitHub, the host of this page, logs when you open it.

What the editor does in your browser

  • MIDI: with your permission, the editor talks to your synth through your browser’s Web MIDI. MIDI data goes only between your browser and the synth.
  • Files: backup files and recordings you open are read in your browser, and the files you save (backups, patches) are made there. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Browser storage: the MIDI channel you choose is kept in your browser’s local storage (hydrasynth-editor.channel), and some dialog choices in its session storage for the current tab. They stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes them.
  • Its own files: the editor loads its wave data, wave pictures and pronunciation dictionary from this same site, nowhere else.

Links to other sites

The editor’s About dialog links to other sites, such as the projects it builds on and their licenses. If you follow one, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example in the country where you live.

I can’t see GitHub’s logs or tell who you are from them, so requests about that data are best sent to GitHub (privacy@github.com). You are welcome to contact me as well.
